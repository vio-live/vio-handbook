# Competitors & Market Landscape

> Last updated: 2026-05-14

The live sports streaming + commerce/engagement overlay market is growing fast — global live commerce exceeds **$1 trillion in 2026**, interactive streaming projected at **$115B+ by 2029**.

**Vio's position**: lightweight SDK that adds real-time commerce overlay to existing sports streams, with a revenue-share model. No platform replacement, no advertising dependency.

## Competitors at a glance

| Company | Model | Stage | Threat level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **StreamLayer** | B2B SDK, advertising/SGAI | $14M raised, ~$2.2M ARR | ⚠️ Medium — similar tech, different model |
| **DAZN** | Platform owner, full commerce | $5B revenue target 2026 | ℹ️ Low — not selling tech, they are the platform |
| **Tivio** | Full OTT platform + commerce | Growth stage | ⚠️ Medium — same broadcasters, heavier product |
| **EaseLive (Evertz)** | Interactive broadcast graphics | Backed by Evertz | ℹ️ Low — broadcast-side, not consumer commerce |
| **Bambuser / Firework** | Live shopping SDK | Series B+ each | ℹ️ Low — retail/fashion, not sports |
