# Competitors — Marketing & Positioning

> Last updated: 2026-05-14

## How Competitors Position Themselves

### StreamLayer
**Their tagline**: "The Video Engagement Operating System (VEOS)"
**Their message**: "Transform live stream viewer engagement through key-moment advertising. Unlock previously unmonetized high-attention moments."
**They talk to**: media companies, sports leagues, OTT platforms
**They promise**: new ad inventory without disrupting the viewer experience

**What they don't say**: that their revenue model competes directly with the broadcaster's existing ad sales team.

### DAZN
**Their message**: "Sports Operating System" — one app for watching, betting, buying, and engaging with sports.
**They talk to**: fans directly (B2C), not broadcasters
**They promise**: everything sports in one place

### Tivio
**Their tagline**: "The Event Operating System for Live Streaming"
**Their message**: "Transform passive viewers into active participants. Embed interactive and monetization layers without touching your existing stack."
**They talk to**: broadcasters, OTT platforms, telcos
**They promise**: new revenue lines without disrupting existing broadcast infrastructure

---

## Vio's Positioning vs Each

### vs StreamLayer
StreamLayer = new ad inventory (CPM model, competes with broadcaster's own ad sales).
Vio = new commerce revenue (revenue share, additive — doesn't touch ad sales).

**Pitch line**: *"StreamLayer gives broadcasters a new way to sell ads. Vio gives broadcasters a new way to sell products — without touching their ad business."*

### vs DAZN
DAZN built commerce for themselves. Broadcasters like Viaplay and TV2 want the same capability but can't build it internally.
Vio is the SDK that gives any broadcaster what DAZN built in-house.

**Pitch line**: *"DAZN spent years and hundreds of millions building live commerce internally. Vio gives your platform the same capability in one SDK integration."*

### vs Tivio
Tivio requires rebuilding or wrapping the OTT stack. 6–12 month integration, high cost, high risk.
Vio is an SDK that drops into the existing app.

**Pitch line**: *"Tivio replaces your streaming infrastructure. Vio adds to it."*

## The Market Narrative (use in pitches)

**The problem broadcasters have**:
Sports audiences are the most engaged and emotionally activated audiences in media. For 90 minutes they are glued to the screen — but broadcasters only monetize through subscriptions and ads. The moment a player scores wearing specific boots, millions of viewers think about those boots. That moment lasts 15 seconds. Today it goes completely unmonetized.

**The solution**:
Real-time product detection + lightweight commerce overlay. The viewer taps, sees the product, buys it without leaving the stream. Broadcaster gets a cut. Brand gets a qualified, high-intent customer.

**Why now**:
- Live commerce globally exceeded $1T in 2026
- Nordic broadcasters (Viaplay, TV2, VG) actively looking for new revenue models
- Mobile-first audiences expect in-app purchasing (Apple Pay, one-tap checkout)
- Affiliate networks (Adtraction) already have the product catalog and tracking infrastructure

**Validated conversion data**:
- Live commerce converts at 9–30% vs <1% for passive display ads
- 71% of viewers trust product recommendations during live content more than online reviews

## Key Messages by Audience

**For Viaplay / TV2 (broadcasters)**:
*"New revenue from your existing audience — no changes to your broadcast setup, no conflict with your ad sales, no upfront cost."*

**For VG (media publisher — article commerce pilot)**:
*"Turn product mentions in your articles into affiliate revenue with a widget that integrates in one afternoon."*

**For Adtraction (affiliate network)**:
*"Your catalog + Vio's overlay = product discovery at the moment of highest intent — live sports."*

**For investors**:
*"StreamLayer raised $14M over 7 years and generates $2.2M ARR with an ad model. We have a commerce model — higher margins, no conflict with broadcaster ad teams, and a $1T market validating the behavior."*
