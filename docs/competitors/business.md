## Market Size

- Global live commerce market: **>$1 trillion in 2026**

## StreamLayer

**The closest structural analog to Vio — B2B SDK for sports streaming**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Total raised** | $14.1M across 7 rounds (1 Seed, 4 Series A, 2 Debt) |
| **Latest rounds** | May 2025: Series A ($500K) + Conventional Debt |
| **ARR** | ~$2.2M estimated |
| **Employees** | ~20 |
| **Founded** | ~2018 |

**Investors:**
- Las Vegas Sands (strategic, May 2022)
- Drive by DraftKings
- KB Partners
- Future of Sport Lab
- Steven Galanis (angel)

**Customers:**
- MLSE (Toronto Raptors, Maple Leafs) — pilot
- Sportsnet / Rogers Sports & Media (Canada) — pilot
- Deltatre (technology partner, DIVA player integration)
- Disney+ (using SGAI technology at scale)

**Revenue model**: CPM advertising. They charge broadcasters/platforms for ad inventory created by their SGAI system. No commerce revenue.

**Business signal**: $14M raised, 7+ years, $2.2M ARR = they haven't found product-market fit at scale. The ad inventory model in sports is hard — broadcasters already have in-house ad sales teams.

## DAZN

**Most advanced live sports commerce in the world — platform owner, not SDK seller**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Revenue target** | $5B by 2026 |
| **Business model** | Subscriptions + advertising + PPV + commerce + betting |
| **Commerce partners** | Fanatics (merch), Daimani (ticketing), ADI Predictstreet (prediction markets), Polymarket |
| **Strategy** | "Sports operating system" — own the full fan relationship |

**Why not a direct threat**: DAZN owns their streams and doesn't sell this technology. Vio is what enables Viaplay/TV2/other broadcasters to do what DAZN does internally.

**Sources:**
- 365247newsletter.substack.com — DAZN sports OS strategy
- sportspro.com — Fanatics e-commerce integration
- globenewswire.com — ADI Predictstreet partnership Apr 2026

## Tivio Studio

**Full OTT platform with commerce for broadcasters and telcos**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Model** | Platform replacement / white-label OTT |
| **Revenue** | Not public |
| **Target customers** | Broadcasters, telcos, pay-TV, rights holders, live promoters |
| **Commerce** | PPV, QR microtransactions, subscriptions, embedded commerce |

**Why relevant**: pitching same buyers as Vio (broadcasters, OTT). Their pitch is heavier — "let us rebuild your app." Vio's pitch is lighter — "add this SDK to what you have."

**Sources:**
- tivio.studio — platform overview

## Vio's Business Differentiation

| | StreamLayer | DAZN | Tivio | Vio |

| Revenue model | CPM ads | Subscriptions + ads | Subscriptions + PPV | Revenue share / affiliate |
| Conflicts with broadcaster ad sales | ✅ Yes | — | Partial | ❌ No |
| New revenue for broadcaster | Partial | — | ✅ | ✅ |
| Capital required to close deal | High (SGAI setup) | — | High (OTT rebuild) | Low (SDK integration) |
| Commerce revenue upside | ❌ | ✅ | Partial | ✅ |
