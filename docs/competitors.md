# Competitors & Market Landscape

> **Purpose**: Keep the team aware of who is operating in the same space — what they do, how they're funded, and where Vio is differentiated. Updated as we learn more.
>
> Last updated: 2026-05-14

---

## The Space

Live sports streaming + commerce/engagement overlay. The global live commerce market exceeds **$1 trillion in 2026**. Interactive streaming segment growing from $32.9B (2024) → projected $115B+ by 2029.

**Vio's position**: lightweight SDK that adds real-time commerce overlay to existing sports streams, with a revenue-share model. No platform replacement, no advertising dependency.

---

## Direct Competitors

### StreamLayer
**streamlayer.io** — closest technical analog to Vio

| | |
|---|---|
| **Model** | B2B SDK embedded in third-party streams |
| **Revenue model** | Advertising (SGAI — Server-Guided Ad Insertion), CPM-based |
| **Raised** | $14.1M across 7 rounds (Seed + Series A + Debt) |
| **ARR** | ~$2.2M estimated |
| **Team** | ~20 people |
| **Investors** | Las Vegas Sands, DraftKings (Drive), KB Partners, Future of Sport Lab |
| **Clients** | MLSE (Toronto Raptors/Maple Leafs), Sportsnet/Rogers, Deltatre, Disney+ (SGAI at scale) |
| **Tech** | Native SDKs iOS/Android/web/CTV, AI event-trigger engine, Google programmatic integration (Feb 2025) |

**Key insight**: StreamLayer went the advertising route — they insert contextual ads at key game moments and monetize on CPM. They have no real checkout, no stock sync, no order management. After 7+ years and $14M raised, $2.2M ARR signals the ad inventory model is hard to scale in sports (broadcasters already have their own ad sales).

**Vio's differentiation**: direct commerce (buy the product, not an ad for it) + revenue share = no conflict with broadcaster's existing ad team.

---

### DAZN
**dazn.com** — most advanced in the world, but not a competitor

| | |
|---|---|
| **Model** | Platform (they own the stream and the commerce layer) |
| **Revenue target** | $5B by 2026 ("sports operating system") |
| **Commerce** | Fanatics merch integration (one-click), ticketing (Daimani), prediction markets (ADI Predictstreet, Polymarket) |
| **Engagement** | Live chat/polls, betting (DAZN Bet), direct athlete interaction (AI-powered translation) |

**Key insight**: DAZN is doing exactly what Vio is trying to enable — but they do it as the platform owner, not as an SDK. They don't sell this technology to other broadcasters. That's the gap Vio occupies.

---

### Tivio Studio
**tivio.studio** — OTT platform with commerce layer

| | |
|---|---|
| **Model** | Full OTT platform replacement for broadcasters and telcos |
| **Commerce** | PPV, QR microtransactions, polls, live stats overlays |
| **Deploy** | "No integration" model — sits on top of existing stack |
| **Target** | Broadcasters, telcos, pay-TV providers, rights holders |

**Key insight**: Tivio wants to replace or wrap the entire OTT infrastructure. Much heavier than Vio. Their pitch is "white-label web/mobile/TV app" — Vio's pitch is "add commerce to your existing app in one SDK integration."

---

### EaseLive (Evertz)
**easelive.tv** — interactive graphics for broadcasters

| | |
|---|---|
| **Model** | Interactive overlay layer synchronized with live stream |
| **Features** | On-stream advertising, polls, trivia, live stats, contextual targeting |
| **Backed by** | Evertz (major broadcast technology company) |

**Key insight**: Focused on broadcast-grade interactive graphics, not consumer-facing commerce. B2B to broadcasters/production houses, not to streaming platforms.

## Adjacent Players (shoppable video, not sports-specific)

| Company | Focus | Model |
|---|---|---|
| **Bambuser** | Live video shopping SDK | Fashion/beauty brands, not sports |
| **Firework** | Shoppable short-form video | E-commerce websites embed |
| **Terrific** | Live shopping platform | Brands host their own streams |
| **Videowise** | Video commerce for e-commerce | UGC + live shopping |

**Key insight**: These players solve live shopping for retail/DTC brands. None have cracked sports. Sports is harder (DRM, latency, rights), but the audience engagement is 10x stronger.
